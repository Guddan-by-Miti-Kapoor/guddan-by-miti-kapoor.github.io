Guddan

Guddan was my mother’s maternal name, a name that became a part of her past after marriage. I wanted to bring it back to life and celebrate the woman who inspired me with her grace, strength and love for timeless traditions.

Carefully curated Banarasi weaves and Lucknowi Chikankari, crafted with love and chosen for women who appreciate elegance, craftsmanship and all things timeless.

With love,Miti Kapoor
